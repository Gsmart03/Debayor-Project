Step 2 — Understand the dataset

Your table has these important columns:

Column	Meaning
order_id	Unique order
order_date	Date of order
customer_id	Customer identifier
product	Product purchased
category	Product category
region	Customer/order region
sales_channel	Where the sale happened
quantity	Units purchased
unit_price	Price per unit
discount_rate	Discount given
revenue	Revenue generated
cost	Cost of the sale
profit	Profit generated
payment_method	Payment method
order_status	Completed, Pending, etc.
